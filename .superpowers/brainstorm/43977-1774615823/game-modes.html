AI Difficulty Levels

These apply to AI mode. Simple/Median/Hard as you described.

🟢 Simple

  • Random moves 80% of time
  • Only blocks/wins when obvious
  • Great for learning

🟡 Median

  • Minimax depth 2-3
  • 20% "noise" factor
  • Balanced challenge

🔴 Hard

  • Minimax + Alpha-Beta
  • Depth 5-7 moves
  • Prioritizes dead boards
